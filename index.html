Wildfire Monitoring Web Application

Deployed

A full stack MERN application, built as both a degree and portfolio project, that pulls real fire-detection data from NASA FIRMS (VIIRS for near real-time data, MODIS for archive data back to 2000) and displays active fires across Pakistan on a live interactive map.

  • Live interactive fire map with 4 map themes and a Pakistan boundary overlay
  • Fire history from 2000 to today, with a time-lapse slider and Chart.js analytics
  • Custom Haversine-based spatial clustering to identify high-risk zones
  • Groq-hosted AI chatbot that answers questions about the fire data
  • JWT auth with OTP email verification, plus a watchlist with proximity email alerts
